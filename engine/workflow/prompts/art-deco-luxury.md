# Style seed: Art Deco Luxury

> Source family: taste-skill §2.B luxury (labeled approximation) · impeccable mode: persuade · dials 9/4/3

## Design Read
Premium/wedding/jewelry/1930s-Gatsby-flavored site — symmetric geometric ornament,
gold-on-dark, fan motifs, stepped arches, high contrast, old-money elegance.
Honest label in code comments: period-inspired, not a museum reproduction.

## Dials
- variance: 9 · motion: 4 · density: 3

## System
- **System:** custom. SVG ornament (thin-line geometric fans/stepped borders) + CSS
  grid symmetry — decorative elements defined once and reused.
- **Type:** high-contrast display serif with geometric quality (NOT Fraunces/Instrument
  Serif) + spaced small-caps labels (tracking-[0.2em]).
- **Color:** deep jade-black or ink base + antique gold (#C9A227 family) + bone.
  Warm-metallic gold is the ONLY allowed luxe metal; no brass-on-cream default.
- **Radius:** sharp (0) structurally; concentric arch/stepped cards (clip-path) for
  ornament.
- **Texture:** thin gold hairline rules, symmetrical ornament bands top/bottom,
  subtle linen/parchment grain.

## Motion (emil bar)
- Slow choreographed entrances (500–700ms hero only), transform+opacity, ease-out
  with a touch of overshoot on the hero mark.
- Hover: ornament stroke lights to gold, 200ms, no blur.
- Symmetric mirror reveals for feature pairs.
- Reduced motion: static ornament, no parallax, no shimmer.

## Bans
Gradients (esp. gold gradients — flat metallic fills only), glass, purple, warm
beige+brass+espresso default, cartoonish shadows, emoji, motion on body text.
