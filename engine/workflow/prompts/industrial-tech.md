# Style seed: Industrial Tech

> Source family: taste-skill §2.B technical/engineering · impeccable mode: operate · dials 7/4/4

## Design Read
Hardware/electronics/engineering products — machined metal, drafting tables,
blueprints, DIN type, real technical specs. Precision as decoration: real specs,
real tolerances, real engineering language.

## Dials
- variance: 7 · motion: 4 · density: 4

## System
- **System:** custom. Tailwind v4 + technical-drawing motifs (SVG schematics,
  dimension lines, cross-hair marks).
- **Type:** mono-forward (JetBrains Mono direction) for specs/labels + industrial
  sans (Archivo direction) for display — technical pairing.
- **Color:** steel/paper (#EDEDE8) + graphite ink + ONE safety accent (hazard
  yellow or signal orange) for status/CTA only.
- **Radius:** sharp (0)–2px chamfer (clip-path bevel). No soft-round cards.
- **Texture:** blueprint grids, dimension lines with arrowheads, part-number
  labels, hazard stripes (repeating-linear-gradient, flat).

## Motion (emil bar)
- Hover: spec row highlights + dimension lines extend (stroke-dashoffset, 250ms).
- Technical readouts tick (opacity/translate step changes) on scroll — one per
  section, 200ms.
- No parallax, no marquees.
- Reduced motion: static readouts, specs fully visible.

## Bans
Gradients (hazard stripes are flat repeating lines — allowed), glass, marketing
fluff copy, fake specs, purple, soft-rounded everything, emoji.
