# Style seed: Organic (Flow)

> Source family: taste-skill §2.B organic · impeccable mode: read · dials 6/5/3

## Design Read
Sustainability/permaculture/organic-food brand — fluid blobs, soil-and-harvest
palette, generous curves, honest materials. Organic LAYOUT, not just organic
shapes: flow, asymmetry, and natural rhythm.

## Dials
- variance: 6 · motion: 5 · density: 3

## System
- **System:** custom. Tailwind v4 + blob masks (chaotic border-radius or SVG blob
  paths) for imagery.
- **Type:** humanist sans + ONE italic serif accent line (not Fraunces/Instrument
  Serif), pairing locked.
- **Color:** soil/oat base (#EFE9DC family) + deep green + one harvest accent
  (tomato, mustard). Earth tones — muted but alive.
- **Radius:** asymmetric organic blobs for images; 16px for cards — two distinct
  scales, documented.
- **Texture:** grain, natural-material photography, thin SVG line-art of
  leaf/fruit motifs.

## Motion (emil bar)
- Blobs breathe once on scroll-into-view (scale 1.02, 900ms, transform+opacity) —
  one moment per page.
- Scroll: gentle fade+rise reveals (300ms), sections only.
- Hover: image blob expands 1.03 + caption rule, 200ms.
- Reduced motion: static blobs, no breathing, content visible.

## Bans
Gradients, neon, glass, plastic-looking 3D, perfect symmetry (organic is
asymmetric), floating shadows, purple, emoji.
