# Style seed: Academia Archive

> Source family: taste-skill §2.B editorial (archival) · impeccable mode: read · dials 7/3/2

## Design Read
Research/library/educational institution site — archival bookish restraint:
index cards, footnotes, margin notes, serif text, catalogue numbers, quiet greens
and ink. Credibility through citation and structure.

## Dials
- variance: 7 · motion: 3 · density: 2

## System
- **System:** custom. Tailwind v4 + CSS Grid with visible catalogue structure.
- **Type:** serif stack for body (NOT Fraunces/Instrument Serif; e.g. Source
  Serif/Literata direction), small-caps labels, mono for catalogue numbers and
  footnotes.
- **Color:** parchment/paper (#F5F1E6 family) + ink + ONE muted accent (hunter
  green, oxblood, or cobalt). Muted, never neon.
- **Radius:** sharp (0), 2px softened paper corners. Hairline rules; index-card
  rows with catalogue numbers.
- **Texture:** paper grain, margin lines, footnote rules, drop caps, marginalia at
  low opacity.

## Motion (emil bar)
- Almost none: hover = underline/rule accent, 150ms; section markers may fade in
  (200ms).
- No scroll choreography, no parallax, no marquees — the reading room stays still.
- Reduced motion: n/a — already static.

## Bans
Neon colors, glass, gradients, motion flourishes, cards-with-shadows, emoji,
decorative difficulty (legibility is the priority).
